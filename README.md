COVID-19 Global Executive Tableau Dashboard Overview
Steps Taken
·      Imported and cleaned the global COVID-19 dataset in Tableau. 
·      Created calculated fields (Vaccination Rate, Case Fatality Rate, Distinct Population, etc). 
·      Built KPI cards and interactive charts. 
·      Combined visualisations into an executive dashboard for analysis. 

Key Findings
·      COVID-19 had a significant global impact, with notable differences across countries and continents. 
·      Vaccination rates increased substantially over time but remained uneven between regions. 
·      Countries with higher GDP per capita generally achieved greater vaccination coverage. 
·      Case Fatality Rates varied, reflecting differences in healthcare capacity, testing, and public health responses. 
·      Africa had the lowest vaccination rate and very high fatality rate in comparison to other regions.

Recommendations
·      Prioritise vaccine equity by targeting countries with the lowest vaccination rate and highest fatality risk.
·      Use the monthly trend to detect rollout fatigue early and trigger booster/catch-up campaigns
·      GDP, population and fatality rates should be used as collective measures for vaccination inequity mitigation.
·      Allocate incremental NGO support to Africa first, especially countries with large populations and low coverage. 
·      Fatality rate as a risk overlay so vaccine support is linked to lives saved, not just doses delivered & use of continent-level scorecard for donor updates

Key Takeaways 
·      Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard analysing global COVID-19 trends. 
·      Created KPI cards, maps, trend charts, and calculated metrics to generate actionable insights. 
·      Identified relationships between vaccination coverage, mortality, and economic indicators. 
·      Demonstrated skills in Tableau, Data Cleaning, Data Visualisation, Dashboard Design, KPI Development, Data Analytics & Storytelling, and Business Intelligence.
